Tokenization is an early step in text preprocessing. A tokenizer splits sentences into words, numbers, or other useful units called tokens. Punctuation removal and whitespace cleanup make those units easier to compare. Case folding converts uppercase and lowercase spellings to a common form. The same normalization should be applied to source documents and user queries. A search index built from clean tokens can match words consistently. Some language tools also remove stop words or reduce related word forms to a stem. The chosen pipeline depends on the document collection and retrieval task.
